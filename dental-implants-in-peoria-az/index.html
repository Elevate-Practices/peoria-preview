Dental implants in Peoria.

Placement and the new tooth, planned and done in one office.

Book onlineCall (623) 877-5700

One office, start to finish.

Many offices send you elsewhere for the implant and back again for the crown. We place the implant and restore it here, so one team plans your new tooth from the first scan to the last visit.

What an implant is.

An implant is a small titanium post that takes the place of a missing tooth root. Once it heals into the bone, it holds a crown, a bridge or a denture that looks and works like natural teeth.

When the bone needs help.

If you've been missing a tooth for a while, the bone can thin. Bone grafting and open or closed sinus lifts rebuild that support so an implant has a solid base.

What we offer.

  • Implant placement
  • Implant crowns and bridges
  • Implant-supported dentures
  • Mini implants
  • Bone grafting
  • Open and closed sinus lifts

Common questions.

How long does a dental implant take?

Most implants take a few months from start to finish, because the post needs time to heal into the bone before the final tooth goes on. We'll give you your own timeline at the consult.

Do I need a bone graft?

Not always. Your scan shows how much bone is there. If it's thin, a graft or sinus lift builds it up first so the implant lasts.

What are mini implants?

Mini implants are narrower posts. They're often used to hold a lower denture steady when there isn't room for a standard implant.

Can I pay for implants over time?

Yes. We offer interest-free payment plans with a flexible down payment, and extended plans. Call and we'll walk you through the options.
