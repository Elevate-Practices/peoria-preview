The new logo

A logo that says implants at a glance.

The new name gets a mark of its own: a crown over an implant.

  • Clear at any size.Two solid shapes and one typeface stay sharp from the sign out front to a browser tab.
  • Dental colors.Trust blue for the crown, clean aqua for the implant, nothing that dates it.
