Common questions.

What happens at my first visit?

We'll take digital X-rays, do a full exam and cleaning, and talk with you about anything you'd like to change.

What should I bring?

Your ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO plans and can use out-of-network PPO benefits. Call to check your plan.

What if I'm nervous?

Tell us. We'll go at your pace, and oral and IV sedation are available.
