Dentures in Peoria.

Full, partial and implant-supported, fitted to you.

Book onlineCall (623) 877-5700

A smile you can count on.

Missing several teeth makes eating and talking harder. A well-fitted denture brings back both, and we'll help you choose the type that fits your mouth and your budget.

Steadier with implants.

A few implants can hold a denture firmly in place, so it doesn't slip when you eat or laugh. We place and restore implants here, so it's one plan with one team.

Flexible partials.

If you're missing a few teeth, a flexible partial with no metal clasps is light, comfortable and hard to spot.

What we offer.

  • Full dentures
  • Partial dentures
  • Implant-supported dentures
  • Flexible partials with no metal

Common questions.

What's the difference between a full and a partial denture?

A full denture replaces all the teeth on the top or bottom. A partial fills the gaps when you still have some healthy teeth of your own.

Can implants hold my denture in place?

Yes. Implant-supported dentures snap onto a few implants, which keeps them from slipping and helps protect the jawbone.

How do I know which denture is right for me?

Come in for an exam. We'll look at your teeth, gums and bone and talk through the options with you.
