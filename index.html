Implants

Implants, start to finish.

Placed and restored in one office, by one team.

  1. Scan and plan.Digital X-rays show the bone and the space, and we plan the new tooth with you.
  2. Build the base, if needed.Bone grafting or a sinus lift gives the implant solid support.
  3. Place the implant.A small titanium post goes in, with oral or IV sedation if you'd like.
  4. Restore the tooth.Once it heals, we fit your crown, bridge or denture here in the same office.

Common questions.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Book online or call us at (623) 877-5700 and we'll find a time that works.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO plans and can use out-of-network PPO benefits. Call to check your plan.

Do you place and restore implants in the same office?

Yes. We place the implant and fit the new tooth here, along with bone grafting and sinus lifts when they're needed.

Can I be sedated for my visit?

Yes. We offer oral and IV sedation for nervous patients, oral surgery and longer visits.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Interest-free payment plans, a flexible down payment and extended plans, plus 5% off when you pay in full up front.
